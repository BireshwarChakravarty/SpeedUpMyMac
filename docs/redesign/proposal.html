Where Root is today

Why it reads as basic

Every page is the same grey card gridIdentical #262628 boxes with text in them. Nothing tells you which section you're in at a glance.
One accent does every jobLime is the button, the chart, the selection and the badge. With one colour there's no hierarchy, and near-black plus one neon pop is the most common template look right now.
No hero objectThe best cleaners give each section a thing to look at. Root has a progress ring and a number, which is what every system monitor has.
The name and icon say "terminal""Root" and a # glyph read as a developer tool or a hashtag. Neither says fast, clean or cared-for.

What changed. The sidebar becomes a floating glass panel with a coloured icon tile for each section, like System Settings. The page takes its section's colour as a soft light behind the content (green-teal here; Cleanup is sky blue, Uninstaller rose). The hero is a glass orb: during a scan, particles of junk drift into it, and Spruce Up sweeps them out with a glint and a sound. Numbers use SF Pro Rounded. Each insight tile carries a small chart. The Sudo switch becomes Admin with a fingerprint, since Touch ID now replaces the password.

Colour world per section

Signature moments

Four moments that get the extra polish

Scan

The orb fills with particles as each place is checked, and the count rolls up. Scanning stops feeling like waiting.

Spruce Up

A swept-glass sound, the particles stream out, and the orb clears to a bright glint. The freed space counts down to zero.

Uninstall

The app icon tilts into the drop tile and dissolves into the section's rose colour. The "Gone without a trace" check follows.

Admin with Touch ID

The Admin switch shows a fingerprint. One touch unlocks system cleaning for five minutes, and the switch glows amber while it's unlocked.

Name and icon

Four names, each with its icon

Each name comes with an icon in the macOS 26/27 layered-glass style. None of them turned up as an existing Mac cleaner in a quick search; a proper trademark check comes before any public release.

Recommended

Spruce

Spruce up your Mac.

"Spruce up" means to tidy and smarten. The icon is three stacked glass chevrons: a spruce tree that also reads as "speed up".

  • Says what it does, in plain English
  • Friendly, short, easy to say aloud
  • The icon carries both cleaning and speed

Halcyon

Calm, clear, fast.

Halcyon days are calm and clear. A sun rising over still water: the feeling of a Mac that's quiet and fast again.

  • Elegant, premium tone
  • Less obvious what it does

Fettle

Keep your Mac in fine fettle.

"In fine fettle" means in great shape, and to fettle is to trim and clean a casting. A polished, faceted gem.

  • Distinctive and ownable
  • Unfamiliar word to many people

Kestrel

Sharp eyes, quick work.

A kestrel hovers still, spots the smallest thing and strikes fast. Swept wings that form an arrow.

  • Speed-first personality
  • Also a well-known web server name among developers
Spruce at Dock and Finder-list sizes. The chevrons stay readable at 32 px.
